Help

Change pages

Swipe left or right inside More Apps. On a computer, drag the page, click the dots, or use the left and right arrow keys.

Arrange your apps

Hold an app, then drag it before another app. The following apps move along in the same order. Hold near the left or right edge to change pages. Tap outside More Apps or tap blank space inside its panel to finish arranging.

Delete an app

In edit mode, tap an app’s minus button. Choose Yes to delete it, or No to return to the same edit page.

Manage pages

Hold an app or an empty slot to edit. Swipe beyond the last page and tap the plus to add a page. On an empty page, use the delete icon in its top-right corner.

Keyboard controls

Focus an app and press M to choose its page and position. Focus an empty slot and press Space to edit pages. Press Escape to finish arranging.
